Description
This dataset focuses on the integration of green energy solutions into traditional village historic buildings while preserving their cultural heritage. It provides key attributes related to building characteristics, renewable energy potential, energy consumption, and optimization metrics.

Key Features
Building Characteristics – Type, year built, material, insulation level, orientation, and floor area.
Energy Consumption – Historical energy demand in kWh.
Renewable Energy Potential – Solar exposure, wind speed, and geothermal capacity.
Optimization Inputs – Factors affecting solar panel installation and energy efficiency.
Performance Metrics – Carbon footprint reduction, cost-effectiveness, and payback period.
Target Variable – Optimal Solar Utilization (Low, Medium, High), determined based on solar potential and installation feasibility.
