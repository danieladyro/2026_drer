Renewable Energy Forecasting

Description:
This dataset focuses on predicting renewable energy output (e.g., solar or wind) under varying environmental and operational conditions. The target variable categorizes energy output levels as Low, Moderate, or High.

Columns (Features):

Feature_1 to Feature_10: Represent factors such as solar irradiance, wind speed, humidity, temperature, panel efficiency, etc.
Target: Renewable energy output class:
Low: Low energy production.
Moderate: Moderate energy production.
High: High energy production.
Business Problem:
Accurate forecasting of renewable energy output helps in efficient energy grid management, storage planning, and mitigating supply-demand imbalances.
